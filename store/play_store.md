## 2. English (en-US)

**App name (30)**: Emlak Deal

**Short description (80)**: Collect properties, charge rent, complete 3 sets first! Play bots or friends.

**Full description (4000)**:

Emlak Deal is a fast-paced property card game about collecting deeds and charging rent. The goal is simple: be the first player to put 3 complete colour sets on your table. Along the way your opponents can steal your properties, hit you with rent and reject your moves, and you can do the same to them.

HOW TO PLAY
• Draw two cards each turn and play up to three.
• Put cards in your bank as money, lay them out as properties, or use them as action cards.
• Collect properties of the same colour: the bigger the set, the higher the rent.
• Add houses and hotels to raise the rent even more.
• The first player with three complete sets wins.

FEATURES
• 2 to 5 players: solo against bots or online with friends.
• Bots on three difficulty levels: Easy, Normal, Hard. Bots talk while they play.
• Online rooms: share a room code, invite friends and play together. Want more on the line? Create a room with an in-game gold stake.
• An 11-colour property collection with real district names from Turkish cities, plus transport and utility cards.
• A 3D table, smooth card animations and custom sound effects.
• Level up, earn XP, finish daily missions and claim your daily reward.
• Spend in-game gold in the shop on card backs and table themes. There are no real-money purchases.
• Achievements, a leaderboard and a friends list.
• No sign-up needed: jump in as a guest. Link Google, Facebook or email whenever you like; your guest progress is added to your account, not lost.
• Turkish and English interface and voices.
• A built-in tutorial teaches the rules step by step.

FAIR AND SAFE
• No ads and no real-money spending.
• Chat messages are filtered for inappropriate words; you can report and block players.
• You can delete your account and all your data from inside the game at any time.

Emlak Deal is an independent game. It is not endorsed by, sponsored by or affiliated with any company or brand.

Questions, ideas or bug reports: sonerozzz@gmail.com

**What's new (500)**: First release. New Pink colour set (Kocaeli), a larger deck and a flow tuned for 5-player games. Your guest progress is now added to your account when you sign in with Google, Facebook or email.
